AlkaLi Labs

Tech Metal Transformation Challenge

AlkaLi Labs is developing engineered biosorbents that recover critical metals from low-grade, chemically complex industrial waste streams. Genetically engineered microbes act as highly selective "metal sponges," enabling recovery at low concentrations where conventional methods fail. The biosorbents tolerate complex contaminants, can be regenerated and reused, and are produced via standard fermentation infrastructure, supporting low-capital, on-site deployment.

ChemFinity Technologies, Inc.

Tech Metal Transformation Challenge

ChemFinity has built a patented porous polymer network (PPN) sorbent platform with exceptional tunability, surface area, and durability, capable of selectively capturing 25+ critical and precious metals from sources including e-waste, catalytic converters, industrial wastewater, and mining residues. The system operates at or near room temperature using modular equipment, enabling rapid, low-energy, low-cost processing with circular reagent use.

Critical Materials Recycling, Inc.

Tech Metal Transformation Challenge

CMR's patented Acid-Free Dissolution Recycling (ADR) is an aqueous, neutral-pH process that selectively recovers rare earth elements under ambient conditions. The integrated system recovers copper from electronic components and converts it into reusable reagents for REE extraction in a closed-loop process, while also recovering secondary metals such as gold and aluminum in market-ready forms.

EDAC Labs

Tech Metal Transformation Challenge

EDAC Labs integrates low-temperature catalytic hydrocracking with hydrometallurgical and electrochemical processing. Hydrocracking removes plastics and converts them into valuable hydrocarbons while preserving metals in extractable forms. Subsequent leaching, electrorefining, and selective precipitation recover high-purity copper, precious metals, REE oxides, tin oxide, and battery-grade materials, while a closed-loop electrochemical system regenerates reagents and produces hydrogen for reuse.

ELSUS

Tech Metal Transformation Challenge

ELSUS is developing selective ion-storage electrodes with electrochemically switchable binding sites. Modules can be activated to capture target metal ions and deactivated to release them, enabling efficient recovery and regeneration in a single system. Complementary electrochemical reactions generate acids and bases in situ for upstream leaching and downstream precipitation, enabling sequential, modular recovery from mixed solutions.

Infinite Elements

Tech Metal Transformation Challenge

Infinite Elements combines BioDissolve™ (low-energy selective dissolution) and BioCapture™ (peptide-functionalized selective capture) into a stepwise platform that enables controlled recovery and transformation of multiple critical metals from heterogeneous feedstocks. From a low-energy, ambient-temperature architecture suited to distributed deployment, the system delivers manufacturing-relevant outputs aligned with U.S. industrial workflows.

Intel-E-Waste LLC

Tech Metal Transformation Challenge

Intel-E-Waste is building a domestic, distributed platform that converts end-of-life electronics into manufacturing-ready materials. The feed-agnostic, modular system tailors processing to specific waste streams (HDDs, PCBs, electronic scrap, solar panels) and integrates targeted disassembly, physical separation, and selective chemical processing to preserve metal integrity and produce clean fractions of aluminum, copper, precious metals, silicon, and REE oxides.

Valor

Tech Metal Transformation Challenge

Valor Metals' proprietary electrochemical liquid-liquid extraction (e-LLE) technology precisely separates individual metal ions from mixed electronic waste streams. The platform enables "direct-to-component" production of high-purity copper foil, gold compounds, REE chlorides and oxides, battery-grade lithium carbonate, and semiconductor-grade gallium oxide, all without large, centralized infrastructure.
